# 📡 IETF Sessions

The IETF VCON working group meets at most IETF plenaries plus interims. Sessions are recorded; minutes, agendas, and slides are posted on the [IETF datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/wg/vcon/meetings/) and stay there permanently.

These recordings are the canonical record of where the spec stands. The working group held no session at IETF 125 (Shenzhen, 2026-03); the datatracker lists none.
